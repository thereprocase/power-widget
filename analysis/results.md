## Range and calibration sensitivity

- ADC current range with 5 mΩ: ±32.768 A nominal, ±32.565 A at the modeled high resistance.
- Retaining an additional 0.5% shunt tolerance raises the 28 V / 5 W allocation to 1.375%; per-unit calibration is a real requirement.
- Connector qualification, thermal gradients, transient response, and long-term drift remain outside this calculator.
